Meeting Board

One-to-one scheduler — teams × decision makers

Saving

Everything you do is saved automatically in this browser — nothing goes to a server. To move the project to another computer, or hand it to a colleague, save a project file and open it there.

Participants

One name per line. Teams become the columns of the interest grid, decision makers the rows. You can come back and add or remove names later without losing the interest you've already recorded.

Slots

How many meeting slots are there across the day(s)? Every participant can have at most one meeting per slot, so this is also the most meetings any one person can have.

Interest grids

Two grids, same layout. Decision-maker interest is the primary driver of the schedule: those meetings are placed first, strongest interest first. Team interest only breaks ties between meetings a decision maker rated equally, and lets a team request a meeting the decision maker didn't ask for (those are placed last).

0 none 1 interested 2 priority 3 must-meet

Generate

Chooses which meetings happen (highest decision-maker interest first, then team interest, spreading equal interest evenly across teams), then fits every chosen meeting into the slots. Generating again replaces any manual changes.

Board

Columns = decision makers, rows = slots. Scroll sideways for the rest. Change any cell to reassign it — picking a team that's already booked in that slot swaps the two meetings. Cell colour shows how keen the decision maker was.

Personal boards

Pick a team or decision maker to see their running order — this is what you'd print or send them.
